# Starter-pack scope

Included:
- binary/family/fine taxonomy and cost profiles;
- validation-only directed vulnerability graph;
- AWBIR, HSR, class-regret and calibration metrics;
- physical Conv1d channel enumeration and surgery;
- magnitude, Taylor, Fisher, BN-gamma and random screening scores;
- Robust Semantic Boundary Leverage;
- resumable single-group causal ablation;
- greedy budgeted selection;
- hierarchical boundary-distillation loss;
- adaptive dual risk controller;
- notebooks 14–18 and synthetic tests.

Not yet claimed as exact paper-baseline reproductions:
- Undecayed pruning;
- FairGRAPE;
- CATRO;
- HALP/FALCON;
- CRISP/N:M.

Those methods require careful paper-specific implementation and validation after
the G1 score gate. The starter pack deliberately avoids labelling approximate
proxies as exact reproductions.
